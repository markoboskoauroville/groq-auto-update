# Skill: gemini-switch-account (Gemini is exhausted: switch the Google account in the same tab)

Marko, 16.11.2026: "teach Queen how to switch accounts when one is exhausted." (He did it by hand during an image job.)
**When:** "gemini is exhausted", "switch the Gemini account", "which Gemini account is active", "image limit reached", and AUTOMATICALLY in every Gemini image job the moment the page shows the limit text.
**Words that mean exhausted (real, 16.11.2026):** "Sorry, I can't generate more images for you today, but come back tomorrow", "You've reached your image-generation limit until 16 Nov, 18:33", try again later/tomorrow, usage limit. NOT the old sidebar chat titles "Image Generation Limit Reached".
**How:** the tool `python3 ~/Developer/MACRO_SKILLS/tools/gemini_account.py <command>` (or POST /api/gemini/account {"do": ...}):
- `status`: every Gemini tab, its account (read from the avatar label), ok / EXHAUSTED / GENERATING, and which accounts are available. Read-only. Always first.
- `switch`: the exhausted tab (the SAME tab, never a second one) goes to the next free account (gemini.google.com/u/N/app, order in ~/.macro_skills/gemini_accounts.json), and the account label is read back.
- `mark-exhausted [email] [--until "16 Nov, 18:33"]`: remember it; the mark ends at Gemini's reset time, else midnight. `next` shows who is next; `scan` re-reads the signed-in accounts (idle tab); `clear` forgets marks.
**THE ORDER (Marko, 16.11.2026: "You switch account under one account and when this is exhausted then go to switching of the profile. Of course avoiding the accounts which are exhausted."):** (1) the next non-exhausted account in the SAME tab of the current profile; (2) when every account of that profile is exhausted, the next Chrome profile (Local State, bottom to top, then wrap) opens as its own window (`open -na "Google Chrome" --args --profile-directory="Profile N" https://gemini.google.com/app`) and its accounts are used; (3) exhausted accounts are skipped everywhere (per email, until the reset time). `switch` does all of it; `switch --no-profile` stops after (1). Everything used up: say when the first resets and stop.
**Rules:** (1) ONE tab, one generation at a time: `switch` refuses while any Gemini tab is generating; wait, never force (parallel tabs got a reCAPTCHA ban, 27.10.2026). (2) Never sign in, never type a password, never click "Add account"; an account that asks "Verify it's you" is skipped by the tool and Marko is told which. (3) Never touch the model; only + -> Create image and the ratio. (4) All accounts exhausted: say "all Gemini accounts are used up, the first one resets at <time>" and stop; the profile step above is automatic. (5) The tab keeps the chat: after the switch start the image prompt again in a new chat.
**Opus gate:** after `switch` the tool prints "label read back OK"; the session still READS the avatar label of the tab (`status`) and checks it names the new account before the job continues. A mismatch or an exhausted new account = another round, never "continue anyway". Every new wording of a limit message goes into the EXHAUSTED pattern of the tool and this file.
